Style: late-1980s and early-1990s Japanese and Taiwanese anime and science-fiction mecha illustration, in greyscale only. Crisp hand-inked outlines, clean cel shading with airbrushed gradients, glossy metallic highlights on machinery, richly detailed biomechanical surfaces. Use only neutral greys from pure black to pure white, with no colour at all. Keep a wide tonal range with deep blacks and bright highlights, and avoid tiny low-contrast noise, because a dither algorithm converts the sheet to a 1-bit image afterwards. Use exactly the same style for every piece on the sheet.

Brightness rule for small projectiles, power-up balls, explosion frames and flashes: the final image is 1-bit and these pieces are drawn over both black space and bright ground. Paint the body light, from mid-light grey to white with a pure white hot core, and surround it with a thin, clean, pure black outline at its edge. Never fill the body dark, and never let the outline fade into grey. The black outline keeps the piece readable on bright ground and the bright body keeps it readable on black space.
