Style: black-and-white line engraving and scratchboard illustration in the manner of late-1980s science-fiction book and technical illustration. Form and tone are built only from black lines on white: parallel hatching that follows the curvature of each surface, cross-hatching for deep shadows, solid black for the darkest areas, bare white for highlights, and a clean confident contour line around every shape. The design and level of detail match a cel-shaded, airbrushed colour illustration of the same subject, redrawn purely as engraved line work. No dots, no screentone, no grey, no gradients, no colour, no anti-aliased smudging. Strictly two values only: pure black #000000 and pure white #FFFFFF.
